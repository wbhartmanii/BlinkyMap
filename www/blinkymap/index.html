🕐 BlinkyMap

What is this device?

BlinkyMap runs as two halves: a control screen that configures the controller and renders the model, and a sensor that carries the camera and compass around the prop. Open one of each.

🖥
Control
Laptop or desktop. Controller setup, session list, 3D model, export. No camera needed.
📱
Sensor
Phone. Camera plus compass — carry it to each position and start the scan from there.
